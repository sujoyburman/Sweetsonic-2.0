WHAT YOU WILL LEARN

More than
DJ controls.

SweetSonic develops musical judgement, technical confidence, creative thinking and performance ability — not just controller operation.

01 / FOUNDATIONS

Beat, BPM & Musical Structure

Understand beats, BPM, bars, phrases, groove and basic musical structure.

02 / TECHNIQUE

Cueing & Beatmatching

Build confidence with cueing, headphone monitoring, tempo control, manual beatmatching and Sync.

03 / MIXING

Transitions & EQ

Perform clean transitions using levels, EQ, filters, faders and musical phrasing.

04 / CREATIVITY

Cues, Loops, Samples & FX

Use performance tools musically rather than mechanically, with purposeful creative decisions.

05 / SELECTION

Build an Energy-Aware Set

Organise music, prepare crates and playlists, sequence tracks and adapt selections to context and audience.

06 / PRODUCTION

DJ + Music Production

Create a simple edit or remix using a DAW such as Ableton Live or Logic Pro and incorporate it into a live mix.

12-WEEK JOURNEY

Learn → Practice → Create → Perform.

Every stage leads toward a practical final performance and portfolio.

01–03FoundationsDJ mindset, controller workflow, cueing and beatmatching.
04–05MixingEQ, levels, phrasing and transition techniques.
06–07Creative DJingHot cues, loops, samples and effects.
08–09Set BuildingMusic selection, energy and audience-aware sequencing.
10–11DJ + ProductionMashups, live edits, MIDI and DAW workflows.
