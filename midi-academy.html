ONE COURSE / MANY SKILLS

Learn the system.
Build your workflow.

MIDI is the bridge between performance, technology and production. We teach the bridge.

01 / DISCOVER

MIDI Fundamentals

Notes, channels, velocity, CC, program change, routing and the language behind modern digital music.

02 / PLAY

Controllers

Keyboard controllers, pads, knobs, faders, pedals and expressive performance techniques.

03 / PROGRAM

MIDI Programming

Quantization, groove, velocity, automation, modulation and creative editing.

04 / PRODUCE

DAWs & VSTs

Hands-on workflows with Logic Pro, Ableton Live, virtual instruments, synthesis and sampling.

05 / ARRANGE

Music Production

Turn MIDI ideas into drums, chords, bass, melodies, arrangements and complete productions.

06 / CREATE

Final Production

Record, edit, mix and master a practical final project that demonstrates the complete workflow.

07 / PERFORM

DJ Performance & Music Mixing

Learn DJ foundations, beatmatching, transitions, creative performance, set building and DJ edits/remixes.
