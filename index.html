SWEETSONIC INNOVATION / BIO TUNE

OUR ASPIRATION · EXPLORATORY CONCEPT IN DEVELOPMENT

Exploring the relationship between sound, frequency & human wellbeing.

BioTune™ is our aspiration to explore how sound, emerging technology and—where appropriate—wearable signals might work together to create thoughtful, user-centred sound experiences.

Discover BioTune™ → Share feedback
01 Explore responsibly 02 Be transparent 03 Follow evidence
SOUND • FREQUENCY • HUMAN EXPERIENCEConcept illustration — exploratory, not a validated medical product.
What we aspire to do

Explore sound-led experiences, learn from user feedback and investigate responsible technology approaches. Any future sensing or adaptive features will depend on design, testing and evidence.

What we are not claiming

BioTune™ is not being presented as a proven treatment, diagnostic tool or cure. No medical benefit is promised; the concept and its capabilities remain subject to development and validation.

A FUTURE POSSIBILITY · NOT A CURRENT CAPABILITY

Imagining a responsive sound experience.

One possible future direction is to explore whether a wearable and software could work together to adapt a sound experience using appropriate, validated inputs. This illustration represents a discussion concept only; it does not describe a built or clinically validated BioTune™ system.

  • Define the intended use and user needs first.
  • Establish sensor, software and safety requirements.
  • Test usability and technical performance before making product claims.

PROPOSED PRODUCT LINE · IN PLANNING

Four connected components. One BioTune™ vision.

These are proposed product directions to guide discovery and requirements planning—not available products, final specifications or confirmed capabilities.

01 / HARDWARE
⌚

BioTune™ Sense

A potential wearable and sensor layer for collecting selected physiological measurements, subject to intended use and sensor evaluation.

  • Heart rate / HRV under evaluation
  • Respiration or EDA may be considered
  • Device connectivity and status
  • Final sensor set remains open
02 / SOFTWARE & AI
✳

BioTune™ AI

A proposed analysis layer to assess supported inputs and explore whether safe, testable sound-personalisation rules can be developed.

  • Signal-quality checks
  • Transparent, testable processing
  • Optional adaptation after validation
  • Fallback when data is unreliable
03 / AUDIO
♫

BioTune™ Audio

A proposed sound-experience layer focused on user-selected audio, straightforward controls and a calm, accessible listening experience.

  • Curated soundscapes
  • Volume and session controls
  • Manual and voice controls to evaluate
  • Easy pause and stop
04 / FUTURE CLINICAL
＋

BioTune™ Clinical

A possible future clinician-facing component, to be shaped with a hospital partner and subject to clinical, privacy and regulatory requirements.

  • Session review and feedback
  • Potential reporting workflows
  • Role-based access and data protection
  • Requirements to be defined with partners

Planning and transparency: These names and functions describe an aspirational product line, not products currently available. No therapeutic benefit is claimed. Sensor selection, algorithms, safety controls, clinical workflows and any medical-device pathway must be defined and appropriately evaluated before clinical use.
