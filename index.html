02 / FREQUENCY

Delta. Theta. Alpha.
Beta. Gamma.

Exploring the science and creative possibilities of frequency — with evidence before claims.

Hz
DELTA~0.5–4 Hz

Associated with deep sleep and studied in neuroscience.

THETA~4–8 Hz

Studied in relation to memory, attention and relaxation.

ALPHA~8–13 Hz

Often associated with relaxed wakefulness.

BETA~13–30 Hz

Associated with active thinking and attention.

GAMMA~30+ Hz

Studied in perception and cognition.

Research note: These are commonly used EEG brain-wave bands. They do not mean that playing an audio tone at the same frequency treats a disease. SweetSonic presents this area as exploratory R&D unless supported by validated scientific and clinical evidence.
